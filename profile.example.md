# What a good fit looks like

Updated: YYYY-MM-DD

- **Roles, in order:** e.g. VP R&D; Head of Engineering; Director of Engineering.
- **Background:** years, last roles, team sizes, the achievements worth matching on.
- **Stack strengths:** e.g. TypeScript, Python, cloud, data, AI.
- **Good signs:** product type, company stage and size, reporting line, city or region, work mode.
- **Weak fits (score low, still mention if strong otherwise):** domains or requirements you don't have.
- **Skip:** things you never want to see (e.g. relocation-only, non-management roles).
